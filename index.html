Klaipeda Live Camera Studio

Klaipeda Live Camera Studio is a personal desktop tool operated by Klaipeda Live Camera (Klaipeda, Lithuania). It prepares timelapse videos and photos of Klaipeda and publishes them to the operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok, after the operator approves them.
