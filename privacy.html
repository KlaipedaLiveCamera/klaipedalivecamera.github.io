Klaipeda Live Camera Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. About this app

Klaipeda Live Camera Studio ("the App") is a personal desktop tool operated by Klaipeda Live Camera (Klaipeda, Lithuania). It is used to prepare timelapse videos and photos of Klaipeda and to publish them to the operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok.

2. Who can use it

The App is used only by its operator, for the operator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and does not create accounts for other people.

3. Data we access

When the operator connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token for that account and basic profile information (display name). The App uses it only to upload the operator's own videos and photos and set their captions and privacy settings.

4. Where data is stored

The access token is stored encrypted on the operator's own computer. We do not run a server, do not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users, and do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. The operator can disconnect the account in the App at any time, which deletes the stored token, and can also revoke access in TikTok settings (Security & permissions > Apps and services).

6. Content shown in videos

The videos are timelapses of a public city view. They are not intended to identify individuals.
